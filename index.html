Where you're getting your water

Two very different starting points.

Plymouth & Canton

Municipal water, GLWA-sourced

Your water is treated and safe to drink — the issue is hardness (7–13 grains per gallon) and chlorine taste, not safety. This is a comfort and appliance-longevity fix, not an emergency.

Salem Township & well-served areas

Private wells, untested by default

Nobody regulates or tests a private well but you. Some problems (iron, sulfur) you can smell. Others (coliform, nitrates, arsenic) you can't — which is exactly why we test before we ever recommend a system.
