About

We started this because the industry doesn't tell you enough.

Most water treatment companies hide their pricing, sell you a rental you never own, and don't explain what's actually wrong with your water. We built Clearwell to do the opposite of all three.

What we test, not guess

We'll test your water on-site free, and if it comes back clean and you don't need anything, we tell you that. Already know what you want? Call and book the install — the test is optional.

What you own, not rent

Flat, published pricing. When the job's done, the system is yours outright — no monthly fee, nothing to hand back.

Where we work

Plymouth, Canton, and the well-water homes west of Napier Road.

We're based in Plymouth and stay close to home on purpose. Salem Township and the well-water homes west of Napier Road get particular attention, since private wells aren't tested or regulated by anyone.
